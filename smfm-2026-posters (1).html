Poster #341

A Physician-Developed Open-Source Web Tool for Cervical Length Screening and Preterm Birth Prevention Counseling

Answer three questions, get evidence-based guidance and a printable patient summary.

You answer

  • Singleton or twin?
  • Prior spontaneous preterm birth before 34 weeks?
  • Transvaginal cervical length (mm) at 18–24 weeks

You get

  • Guidance on vaginal progesterone, cerclage and surveillance
  • A printable patient summary

For patients

  • Plain-language explanations
  • Warning signs to watch for
  • Why the cervical pessary is not routinely recommended in the US

Evidence base

  • SMFM Consult Series #10 (updated 2022)
  • ACOG Practice Bulletin #234 (2021) and PB #130
  • PREGNANT (Hassan 2011), Romero 2012 meta-analysis, PROLONG, Berghella 2011

Why it matters

One in ten US babies is born preterm, and the gap is wider in Georgia and for Black mothers.

10.4%US preterm birth rate · about 380,000 babies · grade D+

Source: March of Dimes 2025 Report Card, 2024 data.
