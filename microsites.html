GDM Screener — Gestational Diabetes Risk Assessment

Interactive clinical decision support tool for determining when to screen for Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM). Step-by-step risk assessment covering BMI thresholds, high-risk factors, and additional risk factors. HTN in Pregnancy — Hypertension Classification Tool

Comprehensive clinical decision support tool for classifying and managing hypertensive disorders in pregnancy. Features blood pressure classification, gestational age-based diagnosis, severe features assessment, and risk factor evaluation for aspirin prophylaxis. Fetal Growth Biometry Calculator — Multi-Standard Percentile Tool

Comprehensive fetal biometry calculator with automated percentile calculations using INTERGROWTH-21st, Hadlock, and WHO growth standards. Features Hadlock formula EFW computation, Doppler indices interpretation (UA-PI, MCA-PI, MCA-PSV), FGR/SGA/LGA classification, and delivery timing recommendations. Includes color-coded results and clinical management guidance.

FGRManager — FGR Surveillance & Delivery Timing Tool

Interactive clinical decision-support tool for managing pregnancies complicated by fetal growth restriction (FGR). Implements SMFM Consult Series #52 delivery-timing thresholds driven by umbilical artery (UA) Doppler category. Provides risk tier, delivery countdown, surveillance schedule, corticosteroid eligibility, BPP alert system, and one-click EMR summary export.
