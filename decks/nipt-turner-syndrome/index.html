Interpreting the Result

Why False Positives Occur

🧬
Confined Placental Mosaicism (CPM)
Abnormal cells present in the placenta but not in the fetus.

Accounts for 59% of discordant Monosomy X NIPT results.
👩
Maternal Mosaicism
Unrecognized maternal Turner mosaicism sheds 45,X cfDNA into maternal circulation.

Mimics a fetal anomaly on NIPT.

A positive NIPT result requires confirmation — it does not establish a diagnosis.

Maternal Counseling

Prognosis & Clinical Features

📊
Prevalence
1 in 2,000 – 2,500 live births.

Confirmed 45,X carries a high rate of spontaneous first-trimester loss.
🧠
Neurocognitive Profile
Intellectual capacity is typically normal.

Specific neurocognitive challenges (visuospatial, executive function) may arise.
🌱
Postnatal Features
Short stature · Ovarian dysgenesis
Delayed or absent puberty · Infertility

Hormone replacement therapy is available and effective.
❤️‍🩹
Lifelong Morbidity
Increased risk of cardiovascular anomalies, autoimmune disease, and metabolic dysfunction across the lifespan.
