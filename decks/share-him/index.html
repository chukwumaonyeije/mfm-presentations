Overview

The Big Idea

🌱
True witnessing is an organic overflow of a living relationship with Jesus
— not a stressful performance.
🤝
Every interaction, even a fleeting moment, is an opportunity
to reveal Christ's love to others.

Sunday

Out of the Overflow

🌐
The Great Commission (Matthew 28:18-20)
We are called to share Christ, backed by His "all authority" and continuous presence.
💬
Personal Testimony (Acts 4:20)
Witnessing is not reserved for eloquent experts — it is the natural result of being an eyewitness to what God has done in your own life.

Monday

Unforced but With Power

Key Concepts
❤️
Jesus was moved with compassion
Matthew 9:36 — His love must be our driving force in reaching souls.
🚪
Coercion goes against God's character
We must invite rather than force, mirroring Jesus' approach to humanity.

Tuesday

Tips for Sharing Jesus

👥
Build genuine friendships and look for natural ways to connect and pray for people's needs.
✝️
Our actions and a consistent, loving character are the strongest arguments for the gospel (1 Peter 3:15).

Wednesday

A Wandering Child

💛
God shares the pain of loved ones who walk away from faith, yearning for them deeply (Jeremiah 31).
🙏
Relinquish control — surrender sadness and frustration to the Lord.
📖
Trust that He is actively working behind the scenes.

Thursday

Bring Them Back

✝️
Stop the debates
Instead, live a consistent life that points others to Christ's unselfish love.
🙌
The testimony of a loving, unselfish life
Coupled with persistent prayer, it has the power to radically change lives.

Inside Story

Stranger in the Parking Lot

📖   Key Moment

Zeth, a volunteer Bible worker in Indonesia, trusted God entirely for provision. After visiting and praying for families daily, God provided miraculously through a stranger in a parking lot when their funds ran out.

A reminder that faithful, sacrificial witness is never unseen — God supplies what He assigns.

✦   Weekly Synthesis   ✦

Key Principles

1

Witnessing flows naturally from a personal relationship with God.

2

Compassion and love must precede any invitation — coercion has no place in ministry.

3

A consistent, unselfish life is the most powerful argument for the gospel.
