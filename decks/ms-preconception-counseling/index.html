Medication Guide

Your Medication Safety Reference

Medication Status Key Action
Glatiramer acetate / Interferon-β ✓ Safe May continue through conception
Natalizumab ⚠ Plan May continue into 2nd/3rd trimester in high-risk patients
Ocrelizumab / Rituximab ⚠ Plan Contraception 6–12 months after last dose; monitor infant B-cells
Fingolimod / Siponimod ✗ Stop 2-month washout required; rebound risk if stopped abruptly
Teriflunomide ✗ Stop Accelerated elimination required; confirm plasma level <0.02 mg/L
Cladribine / Alemtuzumab ✓ Complete First Complete treatment 4–6 months before conception; no further dosing needed

Labor & Delivery

Labor & Delivery — What You Should Know

  • ✔ MS is not a contraindication to any mode of delivery.
  • ✔ Vaginal delivery is generally preferred and encouraged.
  • ✔ All obstetric anesthesia — including epidurals and spinal blocks — is safe and does not increase relapse risk.
  • ⚠ Fatigue may affect the second stage of labor; instrumental assistance may occasionally be needed.
Postpartum

The Postpartum Period — Our Priority

↑
Relapse risk is highest in the first 3 months postpartum
🤱
Breastfeeding may offer modest protection against early relapses
💊
We will plan prompt resumption of your DMT after delivery

High-risk patients may benefit from IVIG or early DMT restart. We will individualize your postpartum plan.

Breastfeeding

Breastfeeding & Medications

Medication Breastfeeding
Glatiramer acetate / Interferon-β ✓ Compatible — large molecules, low infant absorption
Ocrelizumab / Rituximab ⚠ Limited Data — breast milk levels very low; discuss with your team
Fingolimod / Teriflunomide ✗ Avoid — small molecules may transfer into breast milk

Breastfeeding is encouraged. We will select a medication compatible with nursing whenever possible.

Next Steps

Your Preconception Action Plan

  • ① Start folic acid 5 mg daily — at least 3 months before trying to conceive.
  • ② Schedule a neurology review to assess disease stability and plan medication transitions.
  • ③ Confirm 6–12 months of clinical and MRI stability before stopping high-efficacy therapies.
  • ④ Continue regular MFM visits — we will co-manage your care with your neurologist.
  • ⑤ Discuss your postpartum plan now, including medication resumption and breastfeeding goals.
