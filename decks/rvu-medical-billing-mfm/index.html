The visit label is only the starting point.

02

Longitudinal management

Imaging / procedures

Relative value units describe resources, not cash.

01

Work

02

Practice expense

Staff, equipment, supplies and space; values depend on setting.

03

Malpractice

wRVU ≠ total RVU ≠ collections. The employment agreement defines which work receives productivity credit.

01 / Understand value

Payment and compensation use different equations

Keep the payer calculation separate from the employment agreement.

Medicare fee-schedule basis

[(Work × GPCIW) +
(PE × GPCIPE) +
(MP × GPCIMP)] × CF

Each component receives its geographic adjustment. Other payment rules may then apply.

Illustrative employment model

Credited wRVUs
× contract $ / wRVU

Actual agreements may include a base salary, thresholds, exclusions or bonuses.

GPCI = Geographic Practice Cost Index. CF = Medicare conversion factor. PE = practice expense; MP = malpractice.

02 / Describe the encounter

Office MDM requires two of three elements

Medical decision-making reflects what was addressed today.

01

Problems

What condition was assessed? What is its documented status?

02

Data

What qualifying information was reviewed, analyzed or discussed?

03

Risk

What management decisions were made and what risk did they carry?

Meet or exceed two of three elements for the selected level. Use current definitions; do not count separately billed interpretation again as independent interpretation.

02 / Describe the encounter

“High-risk pregnancy” is not an E/M level

Explain the decisions rather than relying on the diagnosis label.

01

Weak documentation

“High risk. BP reviewed. Continue meds.” This does not establish a complete MDM level.

02

More informative

Record BP trajectory, symptoms assessed, condition status, medication decisions and follow-up reasoning.

Why no automatic code here?

A single diagnosis or prescription decision does not supply all MDM elements. The complete record and medically necessary work determine the level.

04 / Physician–APP collaboration

Do not apply one payment percentage to every APP

Medicare allowed-amount examples for covered professional services.

NP / PA

85%

Generally 85% of the physician fee-schedule amount for eligible directly billed services.

CNM

100%

Eligible certified nurse-midwife services have a different Medicare payment basis.

These percentages are not employee pay rates. Medicare’s paid share and beneficiary cost sharing are separate; Medicaid and commercial contracts may differ.

04 / Physician–APP collaboration

Incident-to eligibility requires an established treatment relationship

Medicare office rules require more than an available physician.

01

Treatment involvement

The physician initiated the course of treatment and remains actively involved.

02

Qualifying service

The follow-up fits the established plan and satisfies the other incident-to requirements.

03

Supervision

Required direct supervision and the billing/employment arrangement must be satisfied.

2026 supervision detail

For applicable incident-to services, immediate availability can use real-time audio/video, not audio-only. The 2026 rule excludes services with 010- or 090-day global surgery indicators from this virtual-supervision policy. This does not remove other eligibility requirements.

A new problem or treatment change requires reassessment; do not rely only on “established patient” status.

04 / Physician–APP collaboration

Split/shared E/M belongs to eligible facility encounters

Medicare office visits are not split/shared visits.

01

Eligible setting + team

Physician and APP in the same group; a qualifying facility E/M service.

02

Substantive portion

For noncritical E/M: more than half of combined distinct time OR substantive MDM under the applicable definition.

03

Claim + record

Identify both clinicians; billing clinician signs/dates. Use that NPI and modifier FS.

Count overlapping time once. Critical care uses the time rule. A hospital-owned clinic is not automatically a facility setting; verify actual place of service.

05 / Apply and reconcile

Case: an existing diabetes patient brings a new concern

APP track · fictional scenario · focus on attribution, not clinical treatment.

At the visit

The NP reviews glucose logs, then evaluates a newly reported symptom.

The physician is available but has not evaluated this new problem. The team plans to bill every established-patient visit under the physician.

Reveal the billing reasoning

Do not assume incident-to eligibility for the new problem. Document the NP’s actual work, obtain appropriate clinical input, and reassess the claim pathway. Eligible direct NP billing may be appropriate. A co-signature alone does not resolve the issue.

Clinical escalation follows the patient’s needs. Billing review must not delay necessary care.

05 / Apply and reconcile

Case: FGR imaging and a separate management encounter

Physician track · fictional scenario · fetal growth restriction (FGR).

Two documented activities

The physician interprets the scan, then conducts a distinct management visit.

The visit addresses the implications, surveillance plan and follow-up decisions. The separately reported scan interpretation takes 10 minutes; qualifying E/M work takes 35 minutes.

Reveal the billing reasoning

Evaluate the separate E/M using its supported MDM or 35 qualifying minutes. Verify payer modifier-25 requirements. Do not add the separately reported scan time or count that interpretation again as independent interpretation within MDM.

06 / Take it into practice

Clinical pearls: attribute the work accurately

Eight habits to take back to the clinic.

  1. Start with the service delivered.
  2. Check the payer and actual setting.
  3. Keep wRVUs, payment and compensation separate.
  4. Support the E/M level with MDM or eligible time.
  5. Distinguish scan work from separate management.
  6. Identify who performed each service.
  7. Verify the physician–APP billing pathway.
  8. Reconcile claims and productivity credit.

06 / Take it into practice

Evidence and controversies: identify what governs the decision

Payment policy, payer variation and local compensation are different layers.

01

Established rules

Medicare publishes service and billing requirements; CPT defines coding concepts.

02

Payer-dependent

Consultation recognition, maternity arrangements and contract payment may differ.

03

Unresolved / local

How should team-based care, coordination and protected teaching time receive fair credit?

A useful research question: does better service attribution improve accuracy without increasing documentation burden? This is an educational proposal, not a proven intervention.
