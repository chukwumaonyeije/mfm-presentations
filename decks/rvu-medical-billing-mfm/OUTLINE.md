# RVUs and Medical Billing in the MFM Clinic

Outline for approval | OpenMFM Presentation Standard v1.0 | November 23, 2026

Status: outline approved by the user. Interactive presentation and physician/APP quiz tracks produced in this folder.

## Educational design

**Central message:** Describe the medically necessary service, document the clinician's work, and apply the correct payer and setting rules before calculating productivity or payment.

**Audience:** MFM physicians and MFM APPs, primarily NPs and PAs; identify CNM payment differences when relevant. Assumes familiarity with outpatient high-risk pregnancy care but no formal billing training. Proposed duration: 35–40 minutes plus a 12-question role-specific quiz.

**Scope:** United States outpatient MFM consultation and co-management, diabetes follow-up, maternal disease management, fetal imaging, and physician–APP collaboration. Facility-based outpatient care appears where needed to explain billing boundaries. General gynecology, routine delivery billing, and operative coding are outside the central narrative.

**Six-month takeaway:** Service → setting → payer → rendering clinician → documentation → code → reconciliation.

**Common misconceptions:** RVUs equal dollars; the Medicare conversion factor is an employment-contract rate; referral always means consultation-code eligibility; high-risk pregnancy automatically supports high-level E/M; discussing an ultrasound always supports a separate visit; a physician signature converts APP work into physician billing; all APPs have identical reimbursement rules.

**Learning outcomes:** distinguish wRVUs from total RVUs and collections; explain MFM service attribution; select an appropriate E/M pathway; recognize separate imaging and E/M work; distinguish APP direct billing, incident-to and facility split/shared pathways; reconcile an encounter with the production report.

**Source handling:** The supplied SMFM Career Center article is an introductory narrative, not a coding guideline. CMS rules provide Medicare examples, not universal Medicaid or commercial rules. Date-sensitive material will be labeled for 2026 dates of service; future-year changes will be kept separate. No unverified CPT-specific RVU values or compensation benchmarks will be presented. Fictional calculations and cases will be labeled. Detailed code selection requires the applicable CPT edition, payer policy, and documentation.

## Companion quiz blueprint

**Format:** 16 unique case-based, single-best-answer questions: eight shared questions, four physician questions and four APP questions. A learner selects Physician or APP for a 12-question assessment, or All Questions for all 16. Both roles can review the other track. Four answer choices per question; immediate feedback after submission, rationale for correct and incorrect options, linked sources, and a slide-review link. Score by domain, retry missed questions, restart and a printable answer key. No patient data or claimed CME credit. Scores describe learning, not credentialing.

The following specifies tested decisions rather than final question wording. Exact code-level items will be verified before inclusion.

| ID | Track | Case / task | Intended correct principle | Distractor misconception |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Shared | Identify which RVU component is typically used in a stated productivity contract | Work RVUs, as defined in that contract | Total RVUs or collections equal wRVUs |
