Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build, installation, external scripts, or network connection is required for the presentation itself. Source links require internet access. Typography uses local system fonts with a Space Grotesk preference when available.

- **38 slides:** critical appraisal, patient counseling, clinical pearls, controversies, and complete references.
- **Patient segment:** the toolbar limits navigation to slides 23–32. Print/PDF in this mode includes those 10 slides plus two guideline reference pages.
- **Speaker notes:** click Notes or press N. Complete notes are also in `speaker-notes.md`.
- **Navigation:** arrow keys, Space/PageDown, PageUp, Home/End, slide selector, or horizontal swipe. Shortcuts do not override focused buttons, links, or form controls.
- **Deep links:** `#slide-13` opens the uncertainty chart; `#patient-23` opens the patient segment.
- **References:** click References or press R. Escape closes dialogs. Source buttons on each slide open the cited source.
- **PDFs:** `presentation.pdf` contains 38 pages. `patient-counseling.pdf` contains 12 pages.

The page has no analytics, external assets, patient forms, or saved personal data. The coping-option interaction only changes temporary page text.

## Evidence boundaries

The supplied study PDF and manuscript text support the scientific appraisal. Human placental results are exploratory and cannot establish human brain injury or individual child risk. Raw data and standalone supplemental files were not independently audited. Some source pages were access-restricted; available official guidance supports the clinical counseling, without adding drug-specific algorithms.

The approved 36-slide outline became 38 slides by splitting the final reference section for legibility. See `QA.md` for verification. Local homepage, catalog, and sitemap entries are prepared; publication resources are included in this release.
