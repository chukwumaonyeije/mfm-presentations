05 / Clinical synthesisEvidence appraisal

Clinical pearls: preserve the evidence boundary

  1. Fetal-brain findings are from mice.
  2. The human cohort is exploratory: 20 placentas.
  3. RNA expression is not a measure of functional injury.
  4. Placental mediation remains unproven.
  5. Opposite expression does not establish protection.

05 / Clinical synthesisEvidence appraisal

Evidence and controversies: the causal and clinical links remain open

Known

Exposure-associated RNA changes in this mouse model; exploratory human placental associations.

Uncertain

Timing, persistence, mediation, adaptation versus dysfunction, and individual risk.

Next evidence

Replication with child outcomes; functional tests; clinically meaningful intervention trials.
