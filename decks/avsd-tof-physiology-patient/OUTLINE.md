# Tetralogy of Fallot: understanding your baby's heart

Status: approved for production September 17, 2026. Implemented under OpenMFM Presentation Standard v1.0.

## Educational design

- Audience: patients and families after a prenatal TOF diagnosis; no prerequisite cardiac knowledge.
- Central message: the amount of blood reaching the lungs helps determine the baby's care after birth; a coordinated heart team will personalize the plan.
- Remember in six months: understand the diagnosis, know the birth plan, and keep lifelong heart follow-up.
- Misconceptions: all TOF includes AVSD; fetal stability guarantees newborn stability; all babies need immediate surgery; a screening blood test excludes genetic conditions.
- Decisions: fetal cardiology assessment, optional diagnostic genetic testing, follow-up, birth location, and neonatal support.
- Tone: plain language, realistic hope, acknowledge uncertainty and family preferences.
- Replacement: retain the existing public URL so saved links continue to work. Change its displayed title and catalog description to TOF patient education.
- Preserve accurate AVSD material as a clearly conditional explanation on slide 5, not as an assumed diagnosis in every fetus with TOF. No patient-specific findings or suspected trisomy will be carried forward.

## Production specification after approval

Self-contained semantic HTML, OpenMFM dark palette, plain-language text and labeled original diagrams. Include visible focus indicators, keyboard and horizontal swipe navigation, URL hashes and browser-history handling, progress and slide count, accessible references, reduced motion, mobile scrolling without clipped text, and print/PDF layout. Inactive slides must be absent from keyboard and screen-reader navigation. Diagrams must have equivalent textual descriptions and not rely on color alone.

Verify all claims and citation metadata during drafting; avoid generalized numerical prognosis claims without matching population and outcome definitions. Test desktop, narrow mobile, keyboard-only, deep links, touch, references, and print layout before deployment.
