# Verify the EDD: Gestational Dating for Sonographers and Providers

Outline for approval • October 21, 2026 • OpenMFM Presentation Standard v1.0

## Educational design

**Central message:** At every new visit, verify the menstrual history, retrieve the earliest reliable dating evidence, reconcile the assigned EDD, and document the basis before making gestational-age-based recommendations.

Audience: sonographers, obstetric providers, MFM physicians, and APPs. Prerequisites: basic obstetric biometry and weeks-plus-days notation. Target: approximately 25 minutes plus a 10-question quiz.

The six-month takeaway is a repeatable workflow: **Ask → Retrieve → Reconcile → Document → Communicate.** The workflow and proposed audit measures are educational implementation suggestions, not claims of mandated national practice metrics.

Common misconceptions to address: a recorded LMP is automatically reliable; an outside EDD has already been verified; the most recent scan is the best dating scan; all first-trimester discrepancies use one cutoff; smaller measurements alone establish FGR or pregnancy loss.

Match the recent fetal-pericardial-effusion quiz: 10 single-best-answer questions, four choices each, immediate feedback, reasoning for every choice, relevant slide/source links, final score, full review and retake. ## Corrections and preservation map

- Preserve the original 28-slide deck's accurate themes: LMP limitations, CRL technique, composite biometry, dating thresholds, growth-versus-age distinction, ART, twins, documentation, case reasoning, communication and quality improvement.
- Replace the overlapping first-trimester rows and blanket second-trimester cutoff with the actual ACOG gestational windows.
- Correct the inconsistent ultrasound precision table. Distinguish accuracy ranges from redating thresholds.
- Qualify the absolute third-trimester prohibition: protect reliable early dating, but explain the special situation of a first late scan and its limitations.
- Correct the one-day discrepancy mislabeled as six days. Use exact weeks-plus-days throughout.
- Remove unverified percentages about LMP reliability and unsupported national quality targets. Do not carry forward the original incomplete sentence about pregnancy failure.
- Do not diagnose pregnancy loss, aneuploidy or FGR solely from a dating discrepancy. Escalate according to actual clinical and ultrasound findings.
- Replace the incomplete SMFM reference with identifiable primary guidance. Preserve historical research only if its citation and teaching use are verified during production.
- Explain normal size variation without using ethnicity as a simplistic justification for changing dates.
