Evidence, scope, and sources

The navigator is a structured prompt for quality-improvement and health-system discussions. It does not estimate an individual patient's mortality risk, diagnose conditions, or replace a guideline-based protocol.

How to use the result

  • Review it with local maternal mortality review data, disaggregated by race, geography, and access.
  • Pair it with local referral maps, supply-chain readiness, staffing models, and postpartum access data.
  • Use clinical guidelines and multidisciplinary leadership to define any protocol or patient-care action.

Sources

  • World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet, 2025. Global and regional estimates, cause categories, SDG target, and high-level interventions.
  • WHO/UNICEF/UNFPA/World Bank/UN DESA. Trends in Maternal Mortality 2000–2023. Comparative estimates and regional distribution.
  • Luther et al. Reducing Cardiovascular Maternal Mortality by Extending Postpartum Coverage. JAHA, 2021. U.S. postpartum and cardiovascular context.
