About FGRManager

FGRManager is a physician-facing clinical decision-support tool designed to assist Maternal-Fetal Medicine specialists and obstetricians in managing pregnancies complicated by fetal growth restriction (FGR). It implements the delivery-timing thresholds and surveillance framework from the Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine (SMFM) Consult Series #52.

What This Tool Does

  • Classifies FGR severity based on EFW percentile and umbilical artery (UA) Doppler category.
  • Recommends a delivery window aligned with SMFM thresholds (REDF: 30–32w; AEDF: 33–34w; decreased diastolic flow or severe FGR: ~37w; normal UA with EFW 3–10th: 38–39w).
  • Calculates a countdown from current gestational age to the target delivery window.
  • Generates a surveillance schedule (NST/BPP frequency, UA Doppler interval, growth scan cadence) keyed to Doppler category.
  • Flags antenatal corticosteroid eligibility when preterm delivery is anticipated within 7 days.
  • Flags potentially nonreassuring BPP scores for immediate clinical attention.

What This Tool Does NOT Do

  • It does not replace clinical judgment or individualized patient assessment.
  • It does not account for maternal indications (preeclampsia, HELLP, abruption), fetal anomalies, PPROM, or local NICU capacity.
  • It does not provide a definitive plan — always adapt to the specific patient context.

For Patients

If your provider has shared this tool with you, it means your baby's growth is being carefully monitored. Fetal growth restriction means your baby may be growing more slowly than expected. Your care team uses tools like this to decide how often to check on your baby and when the safest time to deliver might be. Please ask your provider any questions you have about your care plan.
