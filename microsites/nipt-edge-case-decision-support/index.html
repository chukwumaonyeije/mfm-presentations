A clinician-facing teaching tool for NIPT edge cases. It supports counseling pathways for low fetal fraction, abnormal results, multiple aneuploidies, twin/IVF scenarios, and expanded screening. It does not replace clinical judgment, genetic counseling, ultrasound assessment, or diagnostic testing.
