# GDM Screener

## Overview
An interactive clinical decision support tool for determining when to screen for Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM) based on ACOG 2024 guidelines and Practice Bulletin No. 190.

## Features
- **Step-by-step risk assessment** covering BMI thresholds, high-risk factors, and additional risk factors
- **Evidence-based recommendations** for early vs. routine screening timing
- **Clinical reference tables** including OGTT diagnostic thresholds and glucose management targets
- **Dark mode support** for reduced eye strain
- **Educational content** about GDM, its complications, and management
- **Print/PDF export** functionality for patient records

## Clinical Guidelines
Based on:
- ACOG Clinical Practice Update (2024): Screening for Gestational and Pregestational Diabetes in Pregnancy and Postpartum
- ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 190: Gestational Diabetes Mellitus

## Usage
Open index.html in any modern web browser. No server or dependencies required - it's a standalone HTML file with embedded CSS and JavaScript.

## Target Audience
- Obstetricians
- Maternal-Fetal Medicine specialists
- Midwives
- Prenatal care providers
- Medical students and residents

## Disclaimer
This tool is for educational purposes and clinical decision support only. It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. All therapeutic decisions must be discussed with and made by a qualified healthcare provider.
