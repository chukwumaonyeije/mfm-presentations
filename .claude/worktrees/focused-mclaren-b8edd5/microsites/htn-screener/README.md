# HTN in Pregnancy Screener

## Overview
An interactive clinical decision support tool for classifying and managing hypertensive disorders in pregnancy based on ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 222 (2020) and the CHAP Practice Advisory (2022, Reaffirmed 2025).

## Features
- **Comprehensive blood pressure classification** including normal, non-severe, and severe ranges
- **Gestational age-based diagnosis** differentiating chronic hypertension, gestational hypertension, and preeclampsia
- **Severe features assessment** for proper risk stratification and management urgency
- **Risk factor evaluation** for aspirin prophylaxis eligibility
- **Evidence-based treatment recommendations** including medication guidance and delivery timing
- **Dark mode support** for reduced eye strain
- **Educational content** covering types of hypertension, complications, prevention, and treatment
- **Print/PDF export** functionality for patient records

## Clinical Guidelines
Based on:
- ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 222 (2020): Gestational Hypertension and Preeclampsia
- ACOG Practice Advisory (2022, Reaffirmed 2025): Clinical Guidance for the Integration of the Findings of the Chronic Hypertension and Pregnancy (CHAP) Study
- ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 203 (2019): Chronic Hypertension in Pregnancy

## Key Updates
- Incorporates CHAP study findings recommending treatment of BP ≥140/90 mmHg in pregnancy
- Includes aspirin prophylaxis guidelines for preeclampsia prevention
- Addresses superimposed preeclampsia risk in chronic hypertension

## Usage
Open index.html in any modern web browser. No server or dependencies required - it's a standalone HTML file with embedded CSS and JavaScript.

## Target Audience
- Obstetricians
- Maternal-Fetal Medicine specialists
- Midwives
- Labor & Delivery nurses
- Prenatal care providers
- Medical students and residents

## Disclaimer
This tool is for educational purposes and clinical decision support only. It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. All therapeutic decisions must be discussed with and made by a qualified healthcare provider.
